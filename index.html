01 / QUICK HELP

right direction.

A few answers to keep things flowing.

How do I play?+

Drag from a colored endpoint to connect it to a matching endpoint. Plan your routes around the other pipes and complete the board to move on.

Stuck on a puzzle?+

Try a different route, use Undo to rethink a move, or explore the boosters at the bottom of the board.

How do I change the sound?+

Open the settings menu in the game to adjust your audio preferences.

The game froze or closed unexpectedly.+

Close and reopen the app, then check for an app update. If it happens again, tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting: your saved progress is stored on your device.

Do I need an account?+

No. Pipe Dreams opens straight into the puzzle, without an account or sign-in.
